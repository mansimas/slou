# pavyzdziai: example photos for Claude to study

Put photos of real pieces here (your own work, or examples you like), and Claude
looks at them before working on that item in `/editor`. One folder per item:

- `korteline/`: card holder
- `pakabukas/`: keyfob

Any format the browser opens (jpg, png, webp). Filenames can say what the photo shows,
for example `korteline/krastas-is-sono.jpg` or `korteline/kisene-is-virsaus.jpg`.
Photos with a ruler or a card in them are the most useful, because Claude can take real
measurements off them.

Not part of the app: nothing in Sails reads this folder. It is committed and pushed
with everything else, so the photos end up in the public repo.
